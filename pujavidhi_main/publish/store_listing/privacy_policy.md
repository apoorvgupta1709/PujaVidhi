# Privacy Policy for PujaVidhi

**Last Updated: November 2026**

PujaVidhi ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information is handled when you use the PujaVidhi mobile application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
- **Local Application Data**: The App stores your preferences, Japa mala chanting progress, and offline audio files locally on your device using encrypted local storage.
- **Optional Account Data**: If you register or purchase the Bhakt Prime Pass, we associate an anonymous or Firebase Authentication UID to link and restore your lifetime entitlement.
- **Device & Usage Diagnostics**: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic and performance data may be processed through Google Play Services and AdMob to ensure stability.

### 2. How Information is Used
- To provide daily Panchang, Muhurat calculations, and festival rituals.
- To maintain your Japa Mala chanting counter and daily streaks.
- To manage Bhakt Prime Pass entitlements and suppress advertisements for Prime users.
- To enable sharing of customized devotional greeting cards.

### 3. Third-Party Services
The App integrates with select third-party service providers who adhere to strict industry privacy standards:
- **Google Play Services / AdMob**: Provides mobile advertising services for free users.
- **Firebase (Google LLC)**: Cloud database, remote configuration, and storage.
- **Razorpay**: Secure payment processing for the Bhakt Prime Pass. We do not store or process your credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
PujaVidhi is safe for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion
All local data can be cleared at any time by wiping the App's storage via device settings. To request deletion of any cloud-associated profile data, please contact the developer.
